## Georgian-style pork, potato and zucchini bake

For about 3–4 servings:

* 500–600 g pork, cut into 3 cm pieces
* 600 g potatoes, cut into small wedges
* 2 zucchini, cut into thick half-moons
* 1 large onion, sliced
* 4 garlic cloves, crushed
* 1½ teaspoons Svan salt
* 1 teaspoon kharcho spice blend
* 1 teaspoon paprika or chilli flakes, optional
* 2–3 tablespoons oil
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice or tkemali
* Fresh coriander and/or parsley
* Extra tkemali for serving

Heat the oven to **210°C conventional / 200°C fan**.

Mix the pork, potatoes and onion with the oil, garlic, **1 teaspoon Svan salt**, kharcho blend and paprika. Spread everything in a large baking dish or tray. Avoid crowding it too much.

Roast for **25 minutes**, then turn everything.

Meanwhile, toss the zucchini with a little oil and the remaining **½ teaspoon Svan salt**. Add it to the tray and roast for another **20–25 minutes**, until the potatoes are browned and the pork is cooked.

During the final 5 minutes, mix **1 tablespoon tkemali with 1 tablespoon water** and drizzle it lightly over the pork and vegetables. Do not add too much before roasting because the sauce can burn.

Finish with plenty of chopped coriander, a squeeze of lemon and extra green tkemali on the side.

For a more Georgian flavour, add one of these:

* A small handful of walnuts, roughly chopped, during the final 10 minutes
* Thinly sliced fresh chilli
* A pinch of ground coriander
* A spoonful of adjika mixed into the oil

If your pork is **loin or tenderloin**, add it together with the zucchini rather than at the beginning, because it cooks much faster and may dry out.
