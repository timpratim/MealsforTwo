# Easy Beluga Dal Makhani recipe

## Ingredients

* Beluga lentils: 1 cup (uncooked and rinsed)
* Kidney beans: 1 can (15 oz, rinsed and drained)
* Water or vegetable broth: 3 to 3.5 cups (reduced because lentils are pre-cooked)
* Oil or ghee: 2 tablespoons
* Whole cumin seeds: 1 teaspoon
* Onion: 1 medium (finely chopped)
* Garlic: 3 cloves (minced)
* Fresh ginger: 1 tablespoon (minced)
* Fresh tomatoes: 3 to 4 medium round tomatoes (or 5 to 6 Roma tomatoes), pureed in a blender
* Spices: 1 ½ teaspoons garam masala, ½ teaspoon ground coriander, ½ teaspoon chili powder (or Kashmiri mirch for color), and 1 teaspoon salt
* Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves): 1 tablespoon
* The Premium Finish: 2 tablespoons of unsalted butter plus 3 tablespoons of Crème d’Isigny

------------------------------
## Instructions## 1. The Digestibility Step (Your Pre-Cook Method)
Place your rinsed beluga lentils in a pot with plenty of water. Bring to a rolling boil and let them parboil for exactly 1 minute. Drain completely and discard that first water to remove the elements that cause bloating. Return the lentils to the pot with fresh water, bring to a boil, and cook for 20 minutes. Drain any excess water and set the partially softened lentils aside.
## 2. Bloom Cumin & Sauté Aromatics
In your main cooking pot, heat the oil or ghee over medium heat. Drop in the whole cumin seeds and let them sizzle and splutter for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the chopped onion and cook for 4 to 6 minutes until soft and golden. Stir in the minced garlic and ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
## 3. Cook Down the Fresh Tomatoes
Pour in your fresh tomato puree. Stir in the garam masala, ground coriander, chili powder, and salt. Sauté this mixture for 5 to 6 minutes, stirring frequently, until the tomatoes darken and the oil begins to separate at the edges of the pan.
## 4. Combine and Finish the Simmer
Add your pre-cooked beluga lentils, the drained kidney beans, and 3 to 3.5 cups of water or broth. Bring to a boil, then turn the heat to medium-low. Cover and simmer for just 10 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils are completely tender and meltingly soft.
## 5. Mash for Texture
Use a potato masher or an immersion blender to gently mash about a third of the lentils and beans directly in the pot. This instantly thickens the gravy and creates a signature velvety texture.
## 6. The Grand Finale (Kasuri Methi & Cream)
Rub the kasuri methi leaves firmly between your palms to crush them and sprinkle them into the pot to release their smoky, herbal oils. Add the butter and turn the heat down to low. Stir in the Crème d’Isigny until completely incorporated. Simmer gently on low for another 5 minutes to let the flavors meld into a rich, glossy sauce.
Serve hot with garlic naan or basmati rice.
